  Before I write the requirements, one round of questions. Answer in your own words, as much or as little as you like.

  1. **What each skill produces.** What is a tech strategy document to you: for one repository, or for a product or organisation spanning several?   2. **How they chain.** Does an LLD feed into `start-task`, so a spec's `design.md` cites or is derived from it, or does the chain end at the LLD? Does an HLD have to exist before an LLD can be written, and a strategy before an HLD, or can someone start at any level?

  3. **Where the documents live.** Under `.freethinking/` in the repository, like specs, or somewhere else? One file per document, or a folder with sections like a spec?

  4. **"Similar to implement-spec with some reviewers".** Which parts do you mean: a reviewer agent reading each document before you approve it, with findings you see? Sub-agents writing sections in parallel? A cadence choice? And are the reviewers agents, or does the skill also prepare the document for human reviewers?

  5. **Who it is for and the value.** The author writing the strategy or design, a team reading it later, or both? What goes wrong today that these skills fix?

  6. **Out of scope and constraints.** Anything you already know you do not want: no new dependencies is a given, but for example no diagrams beyond Mermaid, no reading of external docs, no ticket integration? Should each skill get evals and a free check like the others, and a self-describing template with a worked example?
