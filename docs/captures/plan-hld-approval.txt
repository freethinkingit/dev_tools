● I wrote two points of the design myself; your answers didn't state them:
  - **Where SQLite lives during the month.** The VM keeps serving `go.internal` through the dual-write month, and Kubernetes takes over only at cutover. SQLite can't sit behind two replicas.
  - **The starting copy.** A one-time copy loads the existing links into Postgres before dual-write begins.
