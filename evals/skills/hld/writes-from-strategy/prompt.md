/freethinking:hld .freethinking/strategies/links-2027.md shortener service. Answers to the design questions: the scope is the strategy's shortener stream, creating short links and redirecting visitors, with visits handed off as events for the analytics stream and nothing of analytics itself; the boundary is the public redirect endpoint and an internal create endpoint, with visitors and the marketing tool outside it and the analytics stream consuming its visit events; the components are an edge redirect handler, a link store in the existing managed key-value database, a create service that writes links, and a visit queue on the cloud's managed queue: a visit hits the redirect handler, which looks up the code in the link store, answers with a 301, and puts a visit event on the queue without waiting for it; key decisions are a 301 rather than a 302, trading exact counts from browser caching for speed, and an asynchronous visit queue rather than counting inline, trading counts a few seconds late for keeping a write off the redirect path; cross-cutting concerns are a 99th percentile redirect under 50 ms, alerts on redirect errors, and no personal data in visit events beyond country and referrer; the risk is a hot link overloading one key, met with a cache in front of the store; out of scope are custom domains and link editing after creation. I will not answer further questions in this run: decide anything else yourself and list it as an open question in the hand-back.
