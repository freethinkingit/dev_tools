# Links 2027

## Introduction and horizon

The links platform takes the Link Shortener from an internal pilot to a product
customers rely on. It serves the marketing teams who share links today and the
partners who want to create links from their own systems. The horizon is January
to December 2027, with a review at the end of each quarter.

## Goals

1. **The shortener in general availability.** Every customer can create and
   resolve short links with redirects under 100 ms at the 99th percentile.
   Worth: the pilot's marketing teams move off the third-party shortener, saving
   its licence by Q3.
2. **Click analytics.** Customers see visits per short link by day, country and
   referrer within five minutes of the visit. Worth: the reason most pilot users
   gave for wanting the product.
3. **A self-serve API.** Partners create and manage short links through a
   documented API with their own keys. Worth: opens the partner channel the
   business plan counts on for the second half of the year.
